# LinkedIn Launch Post

Excited to share that **Lakemeter is now public and open source on Databricks Labs**! 🚀

Lakemeter has been my side project for a while, starting as a small field-built tool within the ASEAN + GCR team to solve a practical problem we kept running into: how do we make Databricks sizing and pricing conversations faster, clearer, and more consistent?

Today, it has grown into a Databricks cost estimation app designed to help teams create more structured estimates for Databricks workloads across clouds and regions.

It runs as a Databricks App, uses Lakebase as the backend database, and supports workload-level cost breakdowns, Excel export, AI-assisted estimate creation, and multi-cloud regional pricing.

For customers and partners, the goal is simple: make it easier to understand, plan, and discuss Databricks consumption before workloads go live.

GitHub: https://github.com/databrickslabs/lakemeter-oss  
5-minute tutorial: https://databrickslabs.github.io/lakemeter-oss/user-guide/getting-started  
Release: https://github.com/databrickslabs/lakemeter-oss/releases/tag/v0.1.0

A big thank you to the contributors who helped build and shape this: Jun Yi Tiong, Chang Shi Lim, and David O'Keeffe.

Special thanks to Deepak Sekar and Vihag Gupta as project sponsors for helping initiate and support the project from the beginning, and to Jason Pohl for supporting the open-source initiative.

This is just the beginning. We are continuing to improve workload coverage, pricing logic, deployment experience, and future distribution options such as Databricks Marketplace.

If you are planning Databricks workloads and want a transparent, deployable way to estimate costs, please try it out, share feedback, and star the repo if you find it useful.
